# Wokot Engine

## Anti-bigotry game engine

Wokot Engine has been forked from Godot to root out bigotry and let developers focus on creativity and gameplay instead of dealing with toxicity after it infiltrates your project.

## Key features

- #### Cross-platform support
  
  Deploy to Windows, macOS, Linux and Android without extra configuration.

- #### MIT license
  
  Like Godot, Wokot is also free and open source.

- #### Community-driven
  
  Wokot Engine is developed by contributors who share a commitment to anti-bigotry.

## Why choose Wokot?

- #### Built-in anti-bigotry
  
  Prevent racist or homophobic content from ever taking hold in your game projects.

- #### True ownership
  
  Your code and assets remain 100% yours. There are no strings attached — except a firm stand against hate.

## Original story

Wokot emerged from a rift in the Godot community chatspaces - "CAFE" and "PLUSH" - where moderation philosophies clashed.

CAFE upheld rigorous anti-bigotry standards, while PLUSH gravitated toward neutrality that inadvertently sheltered harassing voices.
Wokot preserves CAFE's ethos, ensuring that every contributor and user can participate free from threats, slurs or oppressive rhetoric.
